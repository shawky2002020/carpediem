# Public entry and workspace

## Direction exploration

| Direction | How a person would enter and work | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campsite field guide | An original orchard print and official signature introduce the product. A task-led workspace opens onto a dated schedule with a persistent index on desktop. | The illustration can establish place without footage clearance. It must give way to operational information after entry. |
| Dispatch board | Open directly onto a compact wall of notices and shifts. Use high density, persistent queue controls and minimal illustration. | Strong for an administrator on a busy day. Weak as the first impression for a Leader who only needs to respond. |
| Community album | Organize entry around large photographic spreads, people and event stories. Let operational actions sit within each story. | Depends on approved media, identity and event-specific context. The current asset registry and read models cannot support that direction responsibly. |

The field guide combines a recognizable public entrance with a lean operational index. The illustration is new symbolic orchard artwork. The orchard is not evidence of any event venue or a documentary image of the property. The campsite signature and compact badge are the approved project assets. The Drive inventory and outstanding photography clearance are documented in the [asset inventory](../../transformation/ASSET-INVENTORY.md).

Two public-service references informed the hierarchy review. GOV.UK aligns task status with the task itself. California State Parks places event time alongside a concrete activity and place. Carpediem applies these principles to its own event records and action groups. [GOV.UK task list](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/task-list/) · [California State Parks](https://www.parks.ca.gov/)

## Review decisions

- **Welcome.** The original large dark-backed wordmark occupied much of the composition. Rebuilt the entry as a two-part spread with a clear sign-in action, secondary registration link and responsive illustrated print. The first headline sounded like campaign copy. “Your camp workspace” identifies the actual product. [Baseline](baseline/welcome-1440.png) · [Round one](round-1/welcome-1440.png) · [Round two](round-2/welcome-1440.png) · [Round three](round-3/welcome-1440.png)
- **Account access.** Kept a single form column on phones. The shallow orchard band gives continuity without pushing the password field or submit action far below the fold. On desktop, the print and form have independent proportions. The recording exercises invalid email, accessible password visibility, pending registration, success and failed sign-in. [Mobile sign-in](round-3/sign-in-390.png) · [Registration recording](recordings/registration-and-sign-in.webm)
- **Workspace.** A single primary destination follows the role. Other real destinations appear as shorter links. Admin, Chief Leader and Leader home variants were checked at both evidence sizes and at 320 px with enlarged text. [Admin](round-3/workspace-Admin-1440.png) · [Chief Leader](round-3/workspace-ChiefLeader-390.png) · [Leader](round-3/workspace-Leader-390.png)
- **Dashboard.** Replaced a row of independent filters and repeated date tiles with a dated schedule and desktop filter index. Reduced the vertical cost of filters on phones after the second review. In the 390 px review, the first time moved from roughly 524 px down the screen to 402 px. A 200% text regression subsequently exposed an unbroken event title overflowing its column. The dashboard now explicitly permits that title to wrap. [Round two](round-2/operations-390.png) · [Round three](round-3/operations-390.png) · [Desktop](round-3/operations-1440.png)
- **Access denied.** The denial names the permission boundary, keeps a clear return action and remains legible in the same shell as the requesting role. [Mobile](round-3/access-denied-390.png)

The 480 px artwork file is 31,016 bytes. The 880 px file is 76,600 bytes. Public campaigns or campsite photographs with recognizable participants were excluded from runtime.
